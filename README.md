## About Me

I spent decades behind the console—engineering and producing records for artists like Willie Nelson, Dolly Parton, George Strait, Lyle Lovett, Asleep at the Wheel, and the Dixie Chicks. Multiple Grammys, platinum records, film scores—the whole journey.

Now I'm retired and writing software — some of it open source, some of it commercial.

---

## 🎵 Music Production Tools

After a lifetime in studios, I started building tools for fellow musicians:

- **[Larry Seyer Drums](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects/larry-seyer-drums.html)** — The Drum DAW. Four players that sound like real drummers, with organic tempo and arrangement that follows the song. Currently in beta. [Beta Tester Guide](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects/larry-seyer-drums-manual/)
- **[IDA](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects/ida.html)** — Idea Development Arranger. Looping as the capture and repetition of musical ideas — not limited to audio, MIDI, or video
- **[SeyerSampler](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects/seyer-sampler.html)** — A sample-player instrument that uses Morph to generate the missing dynamic layers of a sampled instrument
- **[LOUIS](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects/louis.html)** — The effects engines from Larry Seyer Drums and IDA, as standalone plugins
- **[Sparks of Light](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects/sparks-of-light.html)** — Live MIDI and audio turned into particles and notes on screen, in time
- **[Larry Seyer Video Effects](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects/larry-seyer-video-effects.html)** — Video effects with a proper control panel. Free
- **[MIDI2Button](https://github.com/larryseyer/midi2button)** — Turns MIDI messages into keystrokes

[All projects](https://larryseyer.com/pages/projects.html)
