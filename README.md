## 🎵 About Me

I’m a Grammy Award-winning audio engineer, producer, and musician with over five decades of experience. I’ve had the honor of working with legendary artists such as:

- **Asleep at the Wheel**
- **Lyle Lovett**
- **George Strait**
- **Dixie Chicks**
- **Dolly Parton**
- **Willie Nelson**
- ...and many more

My passion spans the realms of country, Americana, jazz, and beyond. I’ve contributed to major motion pictures, national TV shows, and documentaries—not just as an engineer and producer, but also as a composer.

---

## 🏆 Key Achievements

- 🎤 **Multiple Grammy Awards** for engineering and production
- 💿 **Platinum records** with top-tier artists
